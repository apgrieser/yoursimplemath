What We Offer

  • Differentiated Practice: Three distinct levels of difficulty per topic to meet every student exactly where they are. Answer keys included.
  • Self-Checking Riddles: Engaging puzzles that provide instant feedback—if the answer doesn't make sense, students know to review their work!
  • Visual Learning Tools: Posters and anchor charts to reference difficult algebraic concepts.
  • Answer Keys: Worksheets include answer keys for all difficulty levels.
  • Comprehensive Bundles: Full-unit resource packs designed to save teachers time and money.
  • Targeted Intervention: Perfect for warm-ups, exit tickets, homework, or individual skill-building.
  • Flexibility: Two sizes included for posters: 18x24-inch for wall hanging and 8.5x11-inch for student notes.
  • Ink-Saving Printing: All worksheets and posters include color and black & white versions.

About

Hello, I'm the creator behind Your Simple Math! With over a decade of experience teaching high school math in public schools, I know firsthand the anxiety and frustration students often bring to the classroom.

I understand that a one-size-fits-all approach leaves some students bored and others completely overwhelmed. That's why I am so passionate about differentiation. My goal is to create high-quality resources that help you meet every student at their current skill level, allowing them to build confidence, conquer their math fears, and experience those magical "lightbulb" moments.

Try our free worksheets and posters to see firsthand how our resources can benefit your students, and you!
